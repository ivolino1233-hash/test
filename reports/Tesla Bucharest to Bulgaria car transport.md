# Book a carrier before Tesla Voluntari calls

Several Bulgarian companies say they carry cars from Romania and can be booked from Bulgaria to collect a new Model Y at Tesla Voluntari. The three to contact first are **Евротранс-Петров 2017** (Montana), **cars-transport-bg.com**, and, if you want the whole process handled, **EV Rent / Tesla EVrent Bulgaria** (Sevlievo). Евротранс-Петров owns car carriers and lists Romania as a route. cars-transport-bg.com advertises weekly runs to and from Romania. EV Rent sells a "Tesla from Europe" service for **€2,000**. **Cars-Help**, **KS Trucks** and the Ruse importer **РУСЕ КАРС** are good backups. No company publishes a Bucharest→Bulgaria price. The published figures for longer Western Europe→Bulgaria routes are about **€400 on a shared open carrier** and up to **€950 for a dedicated closed truck from Germany**, so the roughly 364–400 km run from Voluntari should cost less than that ceiling. Tesla's general European practice, as reported on forums, is to release a car to someone else who brings a **power of attorney, a copy of your ID, their own ID and driving licence**, and the order number or VIN. You should still get Tesla Romania to confirm its exact requirements in writing. One Romanian source says a collecting carrier must be "Tesla-approved", and that claim is unresolved. The biggest practical risk is insurance. The standard CMR carrier liability is calculated per kilogram and falls far short of a ~€54k car, so you need full-value cover agreed in writing. **Every contact, price and service detail below comes from search-engine snippets, not pages I could open. Confirm all of it with the company before you rely on it.**

## Tesla releases cars to a stranger with a PoA, but confirm Voluntari's rules

I found no primary text from Tesla Romania on third-party collection, because tesla.com could not be opened. Tesla's FAQ, as quoted on the Tesla Motors Club forum, says someone collecting on your behalf should bring a **power of attorney letter and a copy of your passport or ID card** ([TMC](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/can-someone-else-pickup-the-car.186302/)). Tesla's company-car guidance adds that you should **contact Customer Support in advance** and that the collector needs a **valid driving licence** ([Tesla UK](https://www.tesla.com/en_gb/support/company-car-drivers)). Fleet guidance asks for **government ID plus the order number or VIN** ([Tesla fleet](https://www.tesla.com/support/fleet-drivers)). Tesla's own "Carrier Direct" service, which uses Tesla's carriers, is a separate product ([Tesla AU](https://www.tesla.com/en_au/support/taking-delivery)). The earlier research found a Romanian forum and help-site snippet saying Tesla does not transport cars and that a collecting carrier must be **Tesla-approved** ([Softpedia](https://forum.softpedia.com/topic/1208944-cum-sa-comanzi-o-tesla-in-romania/); [mytes.ro](https://mytes.ro/ajutor/livrarea-comenzii/)). This round found no evidence of that rule. **Ask your Tesla RO delivery advisor directly** whether any licensed carrier is accepted and which PoA format they want. The Voluntari centre (Șoseaua București Nord 15-23) lists hours of **Mon–Fri 08:00–16:30, closed weekends** ([Tesla RO](https://www.tesla.com/ro_RO/findus/list/services/Romania)), so a weekend pickup is not possible.

In practice, the driver should carry the following: a signed PoA, ideally notarised and bilingual BG/RO or BG/EN, naming the driver and the company and authorising them to accept the car, sign the handover and receive the documents; a copy of your ID; the driver's ID and licence; and the order number and VIN. The papers Tesla hands over, the **invoice and COC**, are what you need at NAP and KAT, and the **CMR consignment note** is your best proof of transport for any Romanian VAT refund (see the earlier VAT findings). Agree in advance that all of these come back to you. Also agree that the driver records a photo or video inspection, because you are delegating the damage check that you would normally do yourself at handover.

## Insurance: CMR's per-kg cap falls far short of €54k

Every licensed international carrier works under the CMR Convention, and CMR carrier-liability insurance covers cargo from acceptance to delivery ([DHL BG](https://www.dhl.com/bg-bg/home/tovarni/tsentar-za-pomosht-za-evropeyski-suhopaten-i-zhelezopaten-transport/chesto-zadavani-vaprosi-za-evropeyski-suhopaten-transport.html); [OZK Insurance](https://www.ozk.bg/bg/otgovornost-za-prevozvacha/otgovornost-na-prevozvacha-crm)). The default CMR liability is **8.33 SDR per kg**. For a car of about two tonnes, my rough calculation puts that in the low tens of thousands of euros, well below a ~€54k Model Y. Ask each carrier for its policy certificate, its insured limit per vehicle, and whether it will declare the car's value on the CMR note or buy extra full-value cover, and at what cost. If not, arrange separate cargo or Casco cover yourself from the moment of pickup.

### What to ask every company

| Topic | Question |
|---|---|

| Price | Fixed final price in EUR to [your city], with VAT, tolls, Romanian rovinieta and the Giurgiu–Ruse bridge included? Payment terms? |
| CMR | Will you issue a CMR consignment note showing the VIN, Tesla Romania as sender and me as consignee, and give me a copy? |

| Open vs closed | Open or closed trailer? Upper or lower deck? Price for closed transport? |
| Tesla handover | Have you collected from Tesla before? What PoA wording do you need? Will the driver inspect the car, photograph it, sign the handover, and bring back the invoice, COC and all keys and cards? |
| Licence and identity | Legal name, ЕИК, transport licence number? |
| Extras (agents) | Do you also prepare and file the NAP Appendix 19 VAT declaration and register the car at KAT? What does each cost? |

## Conclusion

Finding a truck is the easy part, because at least four Bulgarian carriers say they already run to Romania. The things that need settling in writing are the ones the carrier sites never mention: Tesla Romania's actual rule on third-party collection, and full-value insurance for a car worth several times the default CMR liability. Get Tesla's written answer on collection first, then compare quotes on insurance terms and the paperwork they return, not only on price. A CMR note with the VIN and Tesla Romania as sender serves twice: it proves the delivery if the car is damaged, and it is the transport evidence Tesla's VAT team will want before refunding any Romanian VAT. EV Rent's €2,000 makes sense only if it includes the transport, NAP and KAT work. If it covers just ordering support, a carrier costing a few hundred euros plus a morning at NAP and KAT costs far less.
