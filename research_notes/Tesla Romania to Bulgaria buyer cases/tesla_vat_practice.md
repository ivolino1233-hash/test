# Tesla VAT practice for cross-border EU buyers of new cars (focus: Tesla Romania -> Bulgarian buyer)

Method note: WebFetch was blocked for every relevant domain I tried: tesla.com, teslamotorsclub.com, kirichkov.com, goelectric.bg, forum.softpedia.com and mytes.ro. Everything below that comes from those sites is based on **search-engine snippets/summaries only**, not full-page reads, so details may be out of context. Dates of forum posts were usually not visible. TMC thread IDs suggest that most of the Tesla-Germany/Netherlands export threads date from about 2019–2023, which is **older than the preferred 2024–2026 window**. Thread 330567 ("after 7.7.2024") is the exception and is from 2024. Recent (2024–2026), well-documented Tesla-RO-specific buyer reports were **not found**.

## 1. Can a Bulgarian buyer get a VAT-free (intra-community) invoice from Tesla Romania, or is it "pay RO VAT, then refund"?

### Takeaway
Under Romanian law, a new means of transport shipped to another member state is an exempt intra-community supply even to a private individual with no VAT number. A Bulgarian blog report on buying from Tesla Bucharest nevertheless describes a "pay Romanian VAT to Tesla, then recover it" process. Tesla's general European practice reported by buyers is inconsistent. Tesla NL historically invoiced net but took a deposit equal to the VAT. Tesla DE/AT historically refused net invoices or only refunded VAT after proof, and in some cases the "VAT manager" refused even then.

### Cited Findings
- Romanian rule: a new vehicle sold to another member state can be treated as an intra-EU supply exempt with right of deduction. The exemption applies to new vehicles **even if the beneficiary does not provide a valid VAT code**. The invoice should carry "scutit cu drept de deducere" and is reported in the recapitulative statement (Form 390). — [infotva.manager.ro: TVA livrare intracomunitara mijloc de transport](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/infotva/tva-livrare-intracomunitara-mijloc-de-transport-4019.html); also [ANAF Brasov guide on vehicle VAT](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Brasov/Brasov/TVA_autovehicule.pdf), [ANAF AIC mijloace de transport](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Brasov/Brasov/AIC_mij_transport.pdf), [portalcontabilitate: tratament fiscal livrare intracomunitara de autoturisme noi](https://www.portalcontabilitate.ro/tratament-fiscal-livare-intracomunitara-de-autoturisme-noi-224448.htm)
- EU definition of "new": supplied within 6 months of first entry into service, or 6,000 km or less. The supply is exempt in the seller's state, and VAT is due in the destination state. The seller must document transport, for example with an entry certificate or freight documents showing the VIN. — [BDO Latvia: EU delivery of new vehicles and applicable VAT](https://www.bdo.lv/en-gb/insights/featured-insights/eu-delivery-of-new-vehicles-and-applicable-vat); [European Commission: Buying and selling cars](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/buying-and-selling-cars_en); [Your Europe: VAT when buying a car abroad](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/vehicles/cars/vat-buying-selling-cars/index_en.htm)
- Bulgarian buyer report (kirichkov.com, "Можем ли да си купим нова Tesla от Букурещ, Румъния?"; exact date not visible in snippets, and the site has a May 2024 archive): it is possible to buy a new in-stock Tesla from the Bucharest showroom. The process is "not that simple" but not radically different from buying in Austria or Germany. The snippets describe **recovering VAT ("възстановяване на ДДС") from Tesla Romania**, which implies Romanian VAT was charged first. One required document is a **NAP certificate proving VAT on the car was paid in Bulgaria**. The snippets also say Romanian legislation has no procedure for an individual to reclaim VAT paid to Tesla Romania directly from the state, and that the author asked ANAF via the electronic form. — [Kirichkov.com post](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/); [Kirichkov.com Romania tag](https://kirichkov.com/tag/romania/)
- Tesla Netherlands (older TMC): Tesla NL "will sell you a car without VAT on invoice" but requires a deposit equal to the VAT "as guarantee that you will export the car". It was described as the only Tesla entity issuing net invoices at that time. — [TMC: Buying from Tesla Germany from outside Germany](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buying-from-tesla-germany-from-outside-germany-i-e-export-to-another-eu-country.291529/); [TMC: Can you order from Tesla Netherlands if you are not Dutch?](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/can-you-order-from-tesla-netherlands-if-you-are-not-dutch.177606/)
- Tesla Germany (older TMC): "Tesla Germany doesn't issue invoices without VAT". One suggested workaround is to buy via a German intermediary company. Other posters say you can email Tesla with the order number, invoice details and a statement that the car is for export to request a VAT-free invoice. — [TMC: Export Model 3 from Germany and VAT Refund](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/export-model-3-from-germany-and-vat-refund.161456/); [TMC thread 291529](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buying-from-tesla-germany-from-outside-germany-i-e-export-to-another-eu-country.291529/)
- One buyer reports that after they submitted all required documents, "Tesla's VAT manager didn't authorize it" with no reason given. The poster noted this was not a problem with Volvo, BMW or Mercedes. Another post says the problem is that Tesla is "too inflexible to provide reverse VAT invoice". — [TMC thread 161456](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/export-model-3-from-germany-and-vat-refund.161456/); [TMC thread 291529](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buying-from-tesla-germany-from-outside-germany-i-e-export-to-another-eu-country.291529/)
- Search summary of TMC threads: some Tesla delivery centers can arrange transport via **Tesla-approved vendors** and issue invoices without VAT, provided the buyer later sends **proof of VAT paid in the home country and proof of registration**. — [TMC: Ordering TM3 in other EU now possible](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/ordering-tm3-in-other-eu-now-possible.155001/); [TMC thread 291529](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buying-from-tesla-germany-from-outside-germany-i-e-export-to-another-eu-country.291529/)
- A Tesla Graz staffer reportedly said Tesla "lost money in Europe due to VAT not being properly charged and export papers [not] being returned from customers". This is a likely reason for Tesla's conservative practice, but it is a snippet paraphrase. — [TMC: Processing VAT refund from Tesla Germany](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/processing-vat-refund-from-tesla-germany.174441/)
- A "Problems with new Tesla registration in EU after 7.7.2024" thread exists (2024). Its content could not be read. — [TMC thread 330567](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/problems-with-new-tesla-registration-in-eu-after-7-7-2024.330567/)
- Romania's standard VAT rate rose from 19% to 21% on 1 Aug 2025. This is the amount a buyer would pre-pay if Tesla RO charges VAT and refunds it later. Some aggregators still list 19%, which is outdated. — [TVR Info](https://tvrinfo.ro/romania-intra-in-august-cu-tva-de-21-si-accize-mai-mari-scumpiri-la-transport-comunicatii-si-alimente/); [fiscalitatea.ro](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/majorare-tva-din-1-august-2025-ce-cote-se-aplica-pentru-avansuri-exemple-utile-24307/)

### Inferences
- Legally, Tesla RO *may* invoice VAT-exempt to a Bulgarian private buyer. In practice, the most realistic expectation is "pay 21% RO VAT (or an equal deposit) up front, then get it back from Tesla RO after you submit Bulgarian VAT-paid and registration proofs". This matches the kirichkov report and Tesla's documented behaviour elsewhere.
- Because the seller, not the state, holds the refund, the buyer depends on Tesla's internal approval ("VAT manager"). There is a reported precedent of refusal.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 first-hand report confirming whether Tesla RO currently issues a net invoice up front versus charging VAT and refunding it. The exact kirichkov post date could not be verified.
- No Tesla RO written policy or support page text on intra-community supplies was found.

## 2. Does the Tesla Order Agreement / support pages require registration in the country of order?

### Takeaway
Yes, by default. Snippets of Tesla's European Motor Vehicle Order Agreement say the car should be registered in the country shown in the agreement footer before or immediately upon delivery. They also say Tesla may cancel orders it believes are made for resale, including if the car is not registered within 7 days of delivery. Cross-border registration therefore appears to need Tesla's separate agreement through an export arrangement.

### Cited Findings
- Order Agreement (EU version, country=BG link): Tesla asks that the vehicle be "registered locally in the country indicated in the footer of the Order Agreement before or immediately upon delivery". Tesla "may unilaterally cancel any order that [it] believe[s] has been made with a view toward resale of the Vehicle (including if your Vehicle has not been registered within 7 days after delivery)". — [Tesla Order Agreement (en_EU, country=BG)](https://www.tesla.com/en_EU/order/download-order-agreement?country=BG); see also [Tesla GB order agreement](https://www.tesla.com/en_GB/order/download-order-agreement?redirect=no&country=GB&model_code=mp) (snippet only; tesla.com blocked)
- Older TMC: buying from Germany or Austria for export required emailing Tesla to place the order "for export" rather than ordering normally online. — [TMC thread 291529](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buying-from-tesla-germany-from-outside-germany-i-e-export-to-another-eu-country.291529/)
- A goelectric.bg (2026) guide describes ordering from Tesla's European site, choosing a delivery center in a nearby market (Vienna, Bucharest, Budapest or Germany), and then arranging transport to Bulgaria. — [goelectric.bg: Tesla Model Y в България 2026](https://www.goelectric.bg/blog/tesla-model-y-bulgaria-2026)
- Context: Tesla registered **Tesla BGR EOOD** in Sofia (established 18 Feb 2026, owned by Tesla International B.V.) and filed on 28 Apr 2026 for sales and service activities. Job listings followed. As of Aug 2026, goelectric.bg said Tesla still had no store or service center in Bulgaria. — [Drive Tesla Canada](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-bulgaria-expansion/); [Basenor](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/tesla-officially-enters-bulgaria-with-sales-service-filing); [goelectric.bg](https://www.goelectric.bg/blog/tesla-model-y-bulgaria-2026)

### Inferences
- A Bulgarian buyer ordering on the Romanian configurator would, by default, sign an agreement whose footer country is Romania. They should get Tesla RO to confirm the export/intra-EU arrangement in writing (email) before paying, to avoid a breach of the "register locally" clause.
- If Tesla BGR starts selling, ordering in a Bulgarian market setting may remove the problem entirely. Whether that has happened by Oct 2026 was not confirmed.

### Gaps
- Full clause text and any explicit export exception could not be read because tesla.com is blocked. The precise wording of the current (2025–2026) RO-footer agreement is unverified.

## 3. Required proofs, VAT refund process and timing

### Takeaway
Proofs reported or required by law are: transport evidence (CMR or entry certificate with VIN), proof that VAT was paid in the destination state (for Bulgaria, the NAP certificate/declaration), and proof of foreign registration. No Tesla-specific 2024–2026 refund timelines were found.

### Cited Findings
- Bulgarian side: an individual files a declaration with NAP (at the permanent-address office) with vehicle data and an invoice copy. The deadline can be as short as 14 days from purchase. VAT on a new car is paid where the car is registered. A certified copy of the filed declaration, or certificate, can take up to 7 days if requested electronically. This NAP certificate is then given to Tesla RO for the refund. — [Kirichkov.com post](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/) (snippet)
- Seller-side proof: entry certificate or freight documents showing the VIN. — [BDO Latvia](https://www.bdo.lv/en-gb/insights/featured-insights/eu-delivery-of-new-vehicles-and-applicable-vat)
- Tesla-reported requirement: proof of VAT paid in the home country plus proof of registration, after which the VAT is returned or not charged. — [TMC thread 155001](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/ordering-tm3-in-other-eu-now-possible.155001/); [TMC thread 291529](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buying-from-tesla-germany-from-outside-germany-i-e-export-to-another-eu-country.291529/)
- Timing: one TMC poster was "in the process of refunding 20% Austrian VAT" with no timeline given. The only formal timelines found apply to *business* VAT refunds under the EU refund directive (4 months, up to 8, then payment within 10 working days). Those do **not** apply to a private buyer's refund from Tesla. — [TMC thread 155001](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/ordering-tm3-in-other-eu-now-possible.155001/); [Lovat: VAT refund in Europe 2026](https://vatcompliance.co/blog/vat-refund-in-europe-2026-and-how-to-claim-vat-paid-in-another-eu-country)
- Business buyers: a VAT-registered BG company providing a valid VIES number, with transport RO to BG proven, gets a VAT-free intra-EU invoice. — [fiscalitatea.ro](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/vanzare-bunuri-in-ue-care-sunt-implicatiile-fiscale-18356/)
- Non-EU (Moldovan) buyers use a different route: export/tax-free, with documents stamped by Romanian customs at EU exit. This is not applicable to Bulgarians. — [infotva.manager.ro tax-free article](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/legislatie/restituire-tva-catre-cumparatori-persoane-fizice-pentru-bunuri-transportate-in-afara-ue-ce-obligatii-fiscale-are-magazinul-tax-free-15219.html); [tv8.md](https://tv8.md/2017/11/04/cum-putem-beneficia-de-restituirea-tva-daca-cumparam-din-tarile-membre-ale-comunitatii-europene) (2017, older)

### Inferences
- The realistic sequence for a Bulgarian private buyer is: pay the full price including 21% RO VAT (or a deposit); take delivery; transport the car to BG; declare it and pay 20% BG VAT at NAP; obtain the NAP certificate; register the car in BG; send CMR or transport proof, the NAP certificate and the BG registration to Tesla RO; then receive the refund. Timing depends on Tesla's internal processing, and no reliable figure was found.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 Tesla RO refund-duration reports were found. No reports were found for Hungarians, Greeks, Poles, Czechs or Croats buying cross-border from Tesla in 2024–2026.

## 4. Transport: approved transporters vs. Romanian temporary plates

### Takeaway
Romanian sources say Tesla RO does not provide transport, and that any third-party carrier collecting the car must be Tesla-approved. Driving away on Romanian provisional plates ("numere provizorii") is a documented option. It requires a 90-day authorization and plates bought on ghiseul.ro plus RCA insurance covering that period. Whether non-residents can obtain them was not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- "Tesla does not provide transport for vehicles; if the owner wants to hire someone, it must be a Tesla-approved company." — [Softpedia forum: Cum sa comanzi o Tesla in Romania](https://forum.softpedia.com/topic/1208944-cum-sa-comanzi-o-tesla-in-romania/) and/or [mytes.ro: Livrarea comenzii](https://mytes.ro/ajutor/livrarea-comenzii/) (snippet summary; exact source line not verified)
- Provisional plates in RO: 13 RON for the provisional authorization plus 40 RON for plates via ghiseul.ro, requested for 90 days. RCA must be valid for at least the plate validity (30/60/90 days). — same snippet set ([Softpedia](https://forum.softpedia.com/topic/1208944-cum-sa-comanzi-o-tesla-in-romania/), [mytes.ro](https://mytes.ro/ajutor/livrarea-comenzii/), [electromobilitate.com 2023 guide](https://electromobilitate.com/ghid-achizitie-tesla-de-la-a-la-z-in-2023-experienta-de-livrare-si-primii-1111-km/) (2023))
- Tesla's Bucharest delivery hub is located at the DAB Auto Serv site in southern Bucharest. — [promotor.ro](https://www.promotor.ro/stiri-auto/in-cat-timp-iti-ajunge-in-fata-casei-o-masina-tesla-pe-care-o-cumperi-de-pe-site-ul-oficial-al-companiei-lui-elon-musk-18714998) (snippet)
- Germany comparison: export plates or short-term plates (Kurzzeitkennzeichen, 5 days, eVB insurance code needed) are described as "a little hard" for foreigners to obtain. — [TMC thread 291529](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/buying-from-tesla-germany-from-outside-germany-i-e-export-to-another-eu-country.291529/); [zoll.de](https://www.zoll.de/EN/Private-individuals/Travel/Entering-Germany/Duties-and-taxes/Purchase-of-motor-vehicles/purchase-of-motor-vehicles.html)

### Inferences
- Romanian provisional plates are normally obtained by the Romanian owner. A Bulgarian buyer most likely needs either a Tesla-approved carrier (which also produces the CMR that supports the exemption or refund) or Bulgarian transit plates. A carrier with CMR is the cleanest proof for Tesla's VAT team.

### Gaps
- The list of Tesla RO-approved transporters was not found. Whether a Bulgarian resident can get RO provisional plates was not confirmed. Whether Tesla RO accepts Bulgarian transit plates as transport proof instead of a CMR was not found.
