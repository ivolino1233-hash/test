## 1. NAP VAT declaration for an intra-community acquisition (ВОП) of a new means of transport: form, payment, deadline, pitfalls

### Takeaway
A Bulgarian private person who is not VAT-registered and buys a new car (no more than 6 months since first entry into service, or no more than 6,000 km) in another EU state owes 20% Bulgarian VAT. They file a declaration (Appendix No. 19 to the ППЗДДС, the VAT Act implementing regulations) at the NAP territorial directorate for their permanent address, and pay within 14 days after the end of the tax period in which the VAT became chargeable. I found no detailed personal account of filing it (queue times, attachments actually requested, payment method).

### Cited Findings
- A car counts as "new" for VAT if it has done no more than 6,000 km, or is delivered within 6 months of first registration. VAT is charged on the full price, including accessories and delivery charges, and is paid in the country where the car is registered — [Your Europe – VAT when buying/selling a car](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/vehicles/cars/vat-buying-selling-cars/index_bg.htm)
- The declaration is in the format of **Appendix No. 19 to the ППЗДДС**. It is submitted to the NAP territorial directorate where the person is registered or should be registered under the ДОПК (Tax and Social Security Procedure Code) — [kik-info.com NAP opinions (snippet)](https://kik-info.com/stanovishta-na-nap/index.php?pageID=15); also cited in the [balans.bg collection "Вътреобщностни придобивания"](https://balans.bg/sbornik/statiya/25037) (snippet)
- A person not registered under the ЗДДС (VAT Act) **files the declaration and pays the tax within 14 days of the end of the tax period in which the tax became chargeable**, at the NAP territorial directorate for their permanent address — [search summary citing ruskov-law.eu, "Данъчно третиране при закупуване и експлоатация на автомобили"](https://ruskov-law.eu/sofia/article/danychno-tretirane-zakupuvane-avtomobil.html). The page itself was blocked, so I could not check the wording.
- Both VAT-registered and non-registered persons, individuals included, must declare a new vehicle acquired from the EU and pay Bulgarian VAT at 20% — [balans.bg](https://balans.bg/sbornik/statiya/25037); [Nula.bg blog, "Облагане на физическите лица с ДДС при покупка на нов автомобил от ЕС"](https://blog.nula.bg/oblagane-s-dds-pri-pokupka-na-avtomobil-kola/) (snippet: an individual without VAT registration who buys a new car in another EU state "must declare the purchase to NAP and pay 20% VAT in Bulgaria")
- **Tesla-specific pitfall (VAT charged twice, then refunded):** a Bulgarian Tesla-community FAQ says a private buyer may have to pay VAT in the purchase country, pay it again at NAP in Bulgaria, and then send Tesla proof of the Bulgarian payment so Tesla refunds the VAT paid in the purchase country — [Tesla клуб България FAQ (search summary)](https://teslaclub.bg/category/faq/). The site did not resolve, the FAQ is undated, and I could not confirm whether this applies to Tesla Romania.
- It is common advice to use an accountant who has done this before — [Tesla клуб България FAQ (search summary)](https://teslaclub.bg/category/faq/)
- Official NAP e-service record for the procedure: [iisda.government.bg service 38](https://iisda.government.bg/adm_services/services/service_provision/38) (blocked; appeared in results for "ново превозно средство … декларация")

### Inferences
- In practice the buyer gets an invoice from the Romanian seller without Romanian VAT (or with VAT that is refunded later, as in the Tesla FAQ), files Appendix 19 at the local NAP office or through the NAP e-portal, and pays 20% by bank transfer to the NAP budget account. The proof of payment and the stamped declaration then go to KAT. This is an inference from the general rules; no source walks through it step by step.
- Since 1 January 2026 the VAT is paid in EUR. I found no source describing a change to the Appendix 19 procedure after the euro switch.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 personal account of filing at a specific NAP office: no waiting times, no record of whether NAP asked for the COC or a translation, no confirmation that e-filing via the PIK code works for Appendix 19.
- Not verified: whether the "14 days after the tax period" deadline matches the current ЗДДС text. The tax period for a non-registered person is the calendar month. The primary law text could not be fetched.
- Not verified: whether NAP issues a separate certificate (удостоверение) for KAT or whether KAT checks the payment electronically.

## 2. Documents KAT required in 2025–2026; invoice translation; VAT proof; technical inspection; green EV plates

### Takeaway
Bulgarian registration guides list these documents for a newly imported car: the foreign registration documents or COC, a contract and/or invoice in the buyer's name, MTPL insurance (ГО), proof of the paid eco/product fee, the owner's ID, and a declaration. One guide says the car must pass a technical inspection (technotest) before registration. I found no source that states plainly whether KAT requires a translation of the invoice or how it checks NAP VAT proof. I could not source green EV plates in Bulgaria beyond a page title.

### Cited Findings
- Documents for a newly imported car: the original foreign registration certificates, the original sale contract plus a copy in your name, the original invoice plus a copy in your name, the product (eco) fee declaration, a vehicle acquisition declaration, MTPL insurance and proof of the paid eco fee. **For new cars the COC is "recommended"** — [search summary aggregating bulcars.bg "Регистрация на автомобил в КАТ през 2025 година"](https://www.bulcars.bg/%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B3%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B8%D0%BB-%D0%B2-%D0%BA%D0%B0%D1%82/), [carapp.bg](https://carapp.bg/registraciya-na-avtomobil-v-kat-dokumenti-taksi-srokove), [autoguardsolutions.bg](https://autoguardsolutions.bg/registraciq-na-avtomobil-v-kat-pulno-rukovodstvo/), [dasweltauto.bg](https://www.dasweltauto.bg/c/blog/dokumenti-za-registraciq)
- "Before registration the car must pass a technotest, and the technical test documents must be filed with the Executive Agency 'Automobile Administration'" — same aggregated search summary (bulcars.bg / autoguardsolutions.bg). **Caution:** this probably describes used imports. A new car with an EU COC is normally registered from the COC without a separate test, but I found no source confirming either way.
- Tesla-focused list of KAT documents for an import: sale contract or invoice, foreign registration documents, customs/VAT document (for non-EU imports), MTPL insurance, ID card — [teslaowners.bg "Регистрация Tesla България" (search summary)](https://teslaowners.bg/%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B3%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%8F-tesla-%D0%B1%D1%8A%D0%BB%D0%B3%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%BE%D1%86%D0%B5%D0%B4%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B0/). The page says KAT registration is "one of the most discussed topics" among owners importing from Germany, the Netherlands, Italy or the USA. **Romania is not mentioned in the snippet.**
- Official MVR service description, "Извършване на първоначална регистрация на превозно средство" (performing first registration of a vehicle), published as a .docx — [mvr.bg](https://mvr.bg/upload/15048/1239-%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%B2%D1%8A%D1%80%D1%88%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B5-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%BF%D1%8A%D1%80%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%87%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B3%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%BD%D0%BE-%D1%81%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B4%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%BE.docx). Found in results; the content could not be read.
- 2026 fees in EUR: regular registration (plates plus certificate) costs about **€31.42**. Fees are converted at 1 € = 1.95583 BGN — [avtoekspert.com, "Цена и разходи за регистрация на автомобил" (2026-01-05)](https://avtoekspert.com/2026/01/05/cena-razhodi-registraciya-avtomobil/) (search summary)
- EV benefits in Bulgaria: no annual vehicle tax and no eco-tax — [teslaowners.bg (search summary)](https://teslaowners.bg/tesla-bgr-eood-balgariya-oficialno-predstavitelstvo/)
- A page titled "Регистрационни табели ППС в България — видове, сини и зелени номера, кодове" (plate types, blue and green numbers, codes) exists — [autostatbg.com](https://autostatbg.com/facts/registratsionni-tabeli-pps). Only the title was visible. It suggests green plates exist, but I have no details on eligibility or price.
- Context: Tesla registered a Bulgarian subsidiary, "Tesla BGR" EOOD (sole owner Tesla International B.V.), on **18 February 2026** — [teslaowners.bg](https://teslaowners.bg/tesla-bgr-eood-balgariya-oficialno-predstavitelstvo/); [digital.bg](https://digital.bg/tesla-naj-posle-vliza-v-balgariya-dokazatelstvoto-veche-e-fakt/); [kaldata.com](https://www.kaldata.com/it-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%BD%D0%B8/tesla-%D0%B5-%D0%BF%D0%BE-%D0%B1%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%BE-%D0%B4%D0%BE-%D0%B1%D1%8A%D0%BB%D0%B3%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B2%D1%81%D1%8F%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%B0-%D0%B2%D0%B5%D1%87%D0%B5-665438.html). This may change how Bulgarians buy Teslas compared with buying from Romania.

### Inferences
- For a new EV, the eco/product fee may not apply, since the sources say EVs are exempt from eco-tax. The core KAT file is then: the Romanian invoice (and contract if there is one), the COC, proof that the VAT was declared and paid at NAP, MTPL insurance, and ID.
- The invoice from Tesla Romania would normally be bilingual (Romanian/English). Whether KAT demands a certified Bulgarian translation could not be confirmed.

### Gaps
- No source confirms whether KAT requires a **translation of the Romanian invoice**.
- No source confirms how **VAT proof** is checked: a physical receipt or declaration, or an electronic NAP–MVR data exchange.
- Not resolved: whether a **technical inspection** is required for a new car with a COC. The snippet suggesting it is required is aggregated and probably refers to used cars.
- Not found: green EV plate rules and price in Bulgaria, or whether they are issued on request.
- No 2025–2026 personal account of KAT registration for a Romania-sourced new car.

## 3. Driving the new car legally from Romania to Bulgaria (temporary/transit plates)

### Takeaway
Bulgaria has transit plates (white with a red stripe). The "T" type is for transit and export, valid up to 30 days, and cost **€40.90** in 2026 according to one guide. Temporary registration plates are issued to traders for one year and to other persons for three years. I found **no source confirming that a Bulgarian private buyer can get Bulgarian plates in advance** to drive an unregistered car bought in Romania. I also found **no source on Romanian provisional plates (numere provizorii) for a Bulgarian buyer**.

### Cited Findings
- Transit plates are white with a red stripe on the right, marked T, H/M or B, and used for transit, unregistered vehicles and dealers. **"T" is for transit/passage and export, valid up to 30 days** — [search summary citing pravatami.bg "Временни, трайни и транзитни номера"](https://www.pravatami.bg/s/3968), [avtoekspert.com "Транзитни номера" (2026-06-30)](https://avtoekspert.com/2026/06/30/tranzitni-nomera/), and [autostatbg.com](https://autostatbg.com/facts/registratsionni-tabeli-pps)
- Temporary registration plates are issued to traders (in the Commercial Law sense) for one year, or to other persons for three years — same search summary ([pravatami.bg](https://www.pravatami.bg/s/3968))
- 2026 fee: **transit registration numbers for export, €40.90** — [avtoekspert.com (2026)](https://avtoekspert.com/2026/01/05/cena-razhodi-registraciya-avtomobil/) (search summary)
- Official iisda service record related to temporary/transit registration: [iisda.government.bg service 21744](https://iisda.government.bg/adm_services/services/service_provision/21744) (blocked; found in results)
- Romanian red temporary plates ("червени временни номера"): a dailydriven.ro article (Bulgarian edition) explains why red temporary numbers are not handed back to police after registration — [dailydriven.ro](https://dailydriven.ro/bg/zashho-cervenite-vremenni-nomera-ne-se-predavat-na-policiiata-sled-registraciiata-1599289) (title only)
- Context for Romania: a BTA report says high Romanian first-registration taxes led Romanians to register cars on temporary plates for the maximum period, then seek Bulgarian registration through companies — [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/bulgaria/86233-Rumanets-izbegnal-plashtaneto-na-danatsi-za-97-avtomobila-e-predaden-na-sada-v) (this is about Romanians using Bulgaria, not Bulgarians buying in Romania)
- If driving through Romania, a Romanian rovinieta (road vignette) is needed; prices are listed per period — [autopay.eu Romania vignettes](https://mobility.autopay.eu/bg/patni-taksi/rumaniya/vinetka-avtomobil-30-dni/)

### Inferences
- In Bulgaria, "T" transit plates are designed for export. Bringing a car in usually means one of two things. Either the buyer uses the seller country's export/provisional plates (in Romania, numere provizorii, which in principle are registered to a Romanian address or through the dealer), or the car is shipped on a car carrier. Neither path for a Bulgarian buyer leaving Tesla Bucharest is confirmed by any source found.

### Gaps
- Not found: whether Bulgarian KAT issues temporary plates (Н/М or В) to a private individual **before** the car is in Bulgaria, and on what documents.
- Not found: whether Tesla Romania or Romanian dealers can arrange provisional (export) plates for a non-resident Bulgarian buyer, or what that costs.
- Not found: an insurance arrangement (MTPL or border insurance) for the drive.

## 5. 2026 changes after euro adoption (fees in EUR)

### Takeaway
KAT fees are now quoted in EUR, converted at 1.95583: registration about €31.42 and transit "T" plates €40.90, according to one 2026 guide. I found no source reporting a substantive change to the NAP Appendix 19 procedure or the KAT documents because of the euro.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 registration costs in EUR: plates plus certificate about €31.42, export transit plates €40.90, vehicle tax from €10.23 (depends on Euro class, year and power), all at 1 € = 1.95583 BGN — [avtoekspert.com (2026-01-05)](https://avtoekspert.com/2026/01/05/cena-razhodi-registraciya-avtomobil/) (search summary)
- Also: ["Документи за регистрация на автомобил" (avtoekspert.com, 2026-06-30)](https://avtoekspert.com/2026/06/30/documenti-registraciya-avtomobil/) (title only)

### Inferences
- EVs are reported as exempt from the annual vehicle tax ([teslaowners.bg](https://teslaowners.bg/tesla-bgr-eood-balgariya-oficialno-predstavitelstvo/)), so the €10.23+ vehicle tax would not apply to a Tesla.

### Gaps
- No confirmation from mvr.bg or nap.bg of the exact 2026 EUR fee schedule; the figures come from a secondary site's snippet.
