## What did Tesla RO actually do on VAT for Bulgarian private buyers and for Bulgarian companies?

### Takeaway
The only account found (Kirichkov) says Tesla Romania **charged Romanian VAT, or initially refused to issue a VAT-free invoice or refund it**. Its advice is to ask up front for a VAT-free invoice and sign a declaration that the car will leave Romania for registration in Bulgaria. If VAT is charged, it says Tesla RO "does refund it, it just needs more pressure", possibly helped by a query to ANAF. I found no account at all covering Bulgarian **company** buyers at Tesla RO.

### Cited Findings
- Kirichkov.com (snippets): when ordering, **ask whether they can issue an invoice without VAT, and fill in a declaration that you intend to take the vehicle out of Romania immediately and register it in Bulgaria**. — [Kirichkov.com](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/)
- Kirichkov.com (snippets): "Tesla Romania may refuse to refund your VAT; don't be shocked: they do refund it, it just takes a bit more pressure." Another snippet says "the people at Tesla claimed they cannot refund the VAT and that one has to seek it from the Romanian state". — same URL; the second wording was also surfaced via [teslaowners.bg](https://teslaowners.bg/tesla-bgr-eood-balgariya-oficialno-predstavitelstvo/)
- Kirichkov.com (snippet): to recover VAT that Tesla RO has not returned, **submit an inquiry through ANAF's (Romanian tax authority's) electronic form** to clarify the procedure by which Tesla can refund the overpaid VAT. — same Kirichkov URL
- Kirichkov.com / goelectric.bg: the **deposit is about €250, paid in Romanian lei** with a bank card added to the Tesla account. — [Kirichkov.com](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/), [goelectric.bg](https://www.goelectric.bg/blog/tesla-model-y-bulgaria-2026)
- **Older TMC precedent from other EU markets (around 2019–2020, not Romania-specific).** Tesla sold cross-border without VAT, but **asked for a VAT-sized security deposit (about €10k quoted) that is refunded after the buyer sends proof of VAT paid in the home country plus registration**. Another variant: the buyer pays home VAT, then sends Tesla the registration documents and VAT payment confirmation to get a refund. Users described the refund process as complicated. — [TMC "Can you order from Tesla Netherlands if you are not Dutch?"](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/can-you-order-from-tesla-netherlands-if-you-are-not-dutch.177606/), [TMC "Ordering TM3 in other EU now possible"](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/ordering-tm3-in-other-eu-now-possible.155001/page-5)
- **Bulgarian side, legal rule.** Anyone not VAT-registered who acquires a **new** vehicle from another EU state must declare it to NAP (the Bulgarian tax agency) on a special form and pay **20% VAT within 14 days**. — [LinkedIn article, "Облагане на физическите лица с ДДС при покупка на нов автомобил от ЕС"](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B3%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B5-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D1%84%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5-%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%86%D0%B0-%D1%81-%D0%B4%D0%B4%D1%81-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%B8-%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%BA%D0%B0-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2-%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B8%D0%BB-%D0%BE%D1%82), [bulgaria-tax-law.bg](https://www.bulgaria-tax-law.bg/vat-new-transport-vehicles.html), [Your Europe FAQ (BG)](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/vehicles/cars/vat-buying-selling-cars/faq/index_bg.htm)
- **A contradictory Romanian general source.** A SmartBill blog says sales to private individuals in other member states are invoiced with Romanian VAT, because the buyer has no VAT ID. This is the general distance-sales rule; it does **not** address the special rule for new means of transport. — [blog.smartbill.ro](https://blog.smartbill.ro/regim-tva-intracomunitar/). By contrast, ANAF has a dedicated leaflet on intra-community acquisitions/supplies of means of transport (found, not fetched). — [ANAF Brașov, AIC_mij_transport.pdf](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Brasov/Brasov/AIC_mij_transport.pdf)

### Inferences
- Under EU VAT Directive Art. 138(2)(a), supplies of new means of transport to **any** buyer, including private individuals, dispatched to another member state are exempt in the seller's country. Tesla RO charging Romanian VAT to a Bulgarian private buyer therefore looks like a front-line sales practice, possibly caused by the buyer collecting the car and driving it out personally, rather than what the law requires. That matches Kirichkov's "they refund it with pressure". This is my legal inference; no source in hand states it for Tesla RO.
- For a **Bulgarian VAT-registered company**, the normal treatment is a 0% intra-community supply invoice against the company's BG VAT ID, with reverse charge in Bulgaria. No first-hand account confirms what Tesla RO actually did for companies.

### Gaps
- I found no account of a Tesla RO VAT-free invoice issued at delivery to a Bulgarian private buyer, and no data on how long a VAT refund took or how much was refunded.
- I found no account of Tesla RO asking for a VAT security deposit (as in older TMC reports from other markets).
- I found no account for Bulgarian companies.

## Which transport method did they use? Any problems at the Giurgiu–Ruse border or with plates?

### Takeaway
I found no concrete account of a specific transport route or a border incident. Sources agree that the buyer has to organise transport. Romanian transit plates are reportedly issued only to Romanian residents and companies and are not valid outside Romania. A Bulgarian buyer would therefore use **Bulgarian transit plates**, which need the Tesla invoice and an origin document, or else use a truck.

### Cited Findings
- Romanian transit plates are issued only to Romanian citizens and to companies registered in Romania, and are not valid outside Romanian territory (snippet, appears to come from the Kirichkov post or goelectric). — [Kirichkov.com](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/), [goelectric.bg](https://www.goelectric.bg/blog/tesla-model-y-bulgaria-2026)
- To get **Bulgarian transit plates**, you need the ownership document (the Tesla invoice) and a "document of origin", per the MVR National Police Directorate (snippet). — same sources
- teslaowners.bg: after delivery at the chosen centre, the buyer "organises transport to Bulgaria". — [teslaowners.bg Model Y guide](https://teslaowners.bg/tesla-model-y-%D0%B1%D1%8A%D0%BB%D0%B3%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%BF%D1%8A%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BD-%D0%B3%D0%B8%D0%B4/)
- Tesla Center București Pipera is at 15-23 Șoseaua București Nord, Voluntari, Ilfov 077190 (sales and delivery). — [tesla.com store page](https://www.tesla.com/findus/location/store/TeslaCenterBucharestPipera)
- Older TMC reports (other markets): cross-border deliveries were first via a Tesla-approved carrier, with third-party carriers allowed later. — [TMC "Can you order from Tesla Netherlands…"](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/can-you-order-from-tesla-netherlands-if-you-are-not-dutch.177606/)
- A Bulgarian registration guide exists on teslaowners.bg (found, not fetched). — [teslaowners.bg Регистрация Tesla България](https://teslaowners.bg/%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B3%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D1%8F-tesla-%D0%B1%D1%8A%D0%BB%D0%B3%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%BE%D1%86%D0%B5%D0%B4%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B0/)

### Inferences
- The likely do-it-yourself path is to get Bulgarian transit plates in advance (or in Bucharest via the BG consulate route; unverified), collect in Voluntari, and drive about 70 km to Giurgiu–Ruse. The alternative is a car-transporter truck. Neither path is documented by a dated first-hand account.

### Gaps
- No reports were found of problems at Giurgiu–Ruse, transport costs, or total time from order to Bulgarian registration.

## Any accounts of Tesla Romania refusing, or requiring specific documents?

### Takeaway
The "refusal" in the sources is about **VAT**: Tesla RO staff reportedly said they could not refund or zero-rate VAT, and the buyer had to push, possibly via ANAF. The document reported as required is a **buyer declaration of intent to export the car and register it in Bulgaria**. I found no source saying Tesla RO refused to sell to Bulgarians.

### Cited Findings
- Tesla RO staff "claimed they cannot refund the VAT and one must seek it from the Romanian state"; Kirichkov says they eventually do refund it "with more pressure". — [Kirichkov.com](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/)
- Required document: a declaration of intent to take the vehicle out of Romania immediately and register it in Bulgaria. — same URL
- Related Romanian rule (the reverse direction, background only): Romania requires proof of VAT payment to register new cars bought elsewhere in the EU. — [Mediafax](https://mediafax.ro/english/registration-of-new-eu-bought-cars-in-romania-to-require-proof-of-vat-payment-7185140/amp)
- Alternative channel: Bulgarian intermediaries (e.g., newtesla.bg, which says it partners with Tesla Germany and Austria) handle ordering, delivery and paperwork. — [newtesla.bg](https://newtesla.bg/en)

### Inferences
- In practice, the documents a Bulgarian buyer would need to show Tesla RO to get the VAT refunded are most likely the Bulgarian NAP VAT payment proof (the VOP declaration for a new vehicle) and the Bulgarian registration certificate, as in older TMC reports from other markets. This is not confirmed for Tesla RO.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025–2026 report of Tesla RO, Greece, Austria or Hungary changing policy toward Bulgarian buyers after Tesla BGR EOOD was registered.
- I found no case for Hungary, Austria or Germany giving a Bulgarian buyer's VAT outcome. Only generic guidance turned up (a German price plus 20% BG VAT; a teslaowners.bg example of about €48,954 for a 2023 Model Y RWD via Germany).
