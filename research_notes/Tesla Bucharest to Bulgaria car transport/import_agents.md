# Bulgarian intermediaries/agents for bringing a new Tesla from Tesla Romania (or another EU store) to Bulgaria

> Method note: WebFetch and curl were blocked by the egress proxy for every target site (tesla.com, kirichkov.com, goelectric.bg, teslaowners.bg, tar.bg, transauto-logistics.com, sosauto.eu). **Everything below comes from search-engine snippets and summaries, not full pages.** Prices and service scope must be confirmed directly with each provider. "Reviews" could not be gathered: no independent review snippets surfaced (Google reviews and Facebook pages were not readable).

## Which Bulgarian services offer pickup of a customer's own Tesla order (from Bucharest or the EU), rather than selling their own stock?

### Takeaway
I found three or four named Bulgarian businesses that advertise help with a customer's own Tesla purchase or import. EV Rent (Tesla EVrent Bulgaria, Sevlievo) is the clearest full-service example, with a stated fee of €2,000. tar.bg sells a "pre-invoicing" route plus a power of attorney so the buyer can arrange their own transport. tesla-service.bg advertises import with transport and document handling. None of the snippets confirmed that any of them works specifically with Tesla Romania (Voluntari/Bucharest). The snippets mostly mention deliveries from Germany or Austria. I found no site called "newtesla.bg". teslaowners.bg is a community and partner hub, and it does not appear to run an import/transport service.

### Inferences
- EV Rent is the only provider I found that advertises a fixed-fee, end-to-end "Tesla from Europe" service and mentions registration and insurance. It is the best first contact for a buyer who wants to place their own order and hand off the logistics. Ask them whether they will collect at Tesla Voluntari.
- tar.bg's model is the reverse: the company becomes the buyer of record, which avoids double VAT, and the customer arranges transport. That fits someone who wants to control transport cost.
- Many generic Bulgarian car-carrier firms could do the Bucharest→Sofia leg, about 364–380 km. That leg is usually a commodity service, separate from the VAT and KAT paperwork.

### Gaps
- No confirmation that any named provider collects specifically from Tesla Romania (Voluntari).
- No reviews or ratings could be retrieved for any provider.
- I could not identify any business called "newtesla.bg". Search returned only shop4tesla.com/bg pages.

## Their fees and what's included

### Takeaway
Only a few figures surfaced: EV Rent charges €2,000 for full order and delivery help; tar.bg charges 1,000 BGN + VAT for pre-invoicing plus a power of attorney; and transport alone runs about €400–950, or €500–900 by another estimate. None of the snippets lists the NAP VAT declaration or KAT registration as a separately priced add-on.

### Cited Findings
- EV Rent: €2,000 for full help with ordering, process consultation and support in Tesla's system. Delivery 7–10 days after Tesla confirms — [evrentbg.com/p/za-nas-9](https://www.evrentbg.com/p/za-nas-9)
- tar.bg pre-invoicing: 1,000 BGN excl. VAT. Includes the order and payment through their company and a power of attorney for transport — [tar.bg](https://tar.bg/)
- Transport: about €400 on a shared car carrier, up to about €950 for dedicated transport from Germany. Tesla Voluntari is about 364 km from Sofia — [search summary citing tar.bg / goelectric.bg](https://www.goelectric.bg/blog/tesla-model-y-bulgaria-2026)
- Transport to Bulgaria: €500–900 depending on distance — [search summary, kirichkov.com / goelectric.bg](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/)
- VAT: for a new car brought in from another EU country, the buyer owes 20% Bulgarian VAT and must file a declaration with NAP within 14 days. The declaration goes to the NAP office for your permanent address, with the vehicle data and a copy of the invoice — [goelectric.bg (snippet)](https://www.goelectric.bg/blog/tesla-model-y-bulgaria-2026)
- kirichkov.com advises that, when ordering in Bucharest, you ask Tesla to invoice without VAT and fill in a declaration that the car will leave Romania immediately and be registered in Bulgaria. It also suggests ordering in person at the Bucharest showroom, because the sales team can help place the order and the store has free Wi-Fi, which matters because adding a Bulgarian card in the app does not always work — [kirichkov.com](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/) (post date not visible; it quotes an older Model 3 price of €42,990, so it is probably several years old)
- teslaowners.bg's insurance partner offers free registration assistance to members in Sofia — [teslaowners.bg](https://teslaowners.bg/)

### Inferences
- A realistic budget for outsourcing everything through an agent is about €2,000 (EV Rent). That may or may not include the ~€400–950 transport. Doing it yourself with a carrier costs roughly €400–950 plus your own time at NAP and KAT.

### Gaps
- No published fee list for doing the NAP declaration or KAT registration by proxy, for plates, or for a Tesla handover inspection by an agent.
- Whether EV Rent's €2,000 includes transport and registration is ambiguous in the snippets.

## Does Tesla Romania require a Tesla-approved carrier, or does it accept any carrier with the owner's authorisation? What does the carrier need at handover?

### Takeaway
I found no Romania-specific rule. Tesla's general practice in Europe, reported on forums, is that anyone can collect if they bring a power of attorney or authorisation letter, a copy of the owner's passport or ID card, and their own ID. Telling Tesla in advance through support or the app is recommended. I found no evidence that Tesla requires a "Tesla-approved" transporter for collections the customer arranges. Tesla's own "Carrier Direct" uses Tesla's carriers, but that is a different, Tesla-arranged service.

### Cited Findings
- Tesla FAQ text quoted on TMC: someone collecting on your behalf should bring a **power of attorney letter and a copy of your passport or ID card** to the delivery appointment — [TMC: Can someone else pickup the car?](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/can-someone-else-pickup-the-car.186302/)
- One user said an email to Tesla authorising the collector was enough, and they were not even asked for a driving licence. Another recommended ID, an authorisation letter, and insurance naming the collector as a driver — [same TMC thread](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/can-someone-else-pickup-the-car.186302/)
- Tesla UK company-car guidance: to have someone else collect, contact Customer Support (and the leasing company). The designated person needs a valid driving licence — [tesla.com/en_gb/support/company-car-drivers](https://www.tesla.com/en_gb/support/company-car-drivers)
- Fleet guidance: the designated point of contact must show a valid government ID and the order number or VIN on delivery day — [tesla.com fleet-drivers (snippet)](https://www.tesla.com/support/fleet-drivers)
- Tesla RO's delivery-day page says to confirm delivery details in the account (location, registration, trade-in, payment) and to bring all required documents. The page was not readable, so I could not check its third-party clause — [tesla.com/ro_RO/support/delivery-day](https://www.tesla.com/ro_RO/support/delivery-day)
- Tesla's "Carrier Direct" (Tesla-arranged third-party carrier) is for customers more than 250 km from a delivery centre, and payment and signed documents are required first. This description comes from non-RO Tesla pages — [tesla.com/en_au/support/taking-delivery](https://www.tesla.com/en_au/support/taking-delivery)
- Tesla Bucharest location: Tesla Service Voluntari, Șoseaua București Nord 15-23, Mon–Fri 08:00–16:30, closed weekends — [tesla.com RO service list (snippet)](https://www.tesla.com/ro_RO/findus/list/services/Romania)
- tar.bg's model gives the customer a power of attorney so a chosen transporter can collect, which supports the view that Tesla accepts collection under a PoA — [tar.bg](https://tar.bg/)

### Inferences
- For a pickup in Bucharest, the carrier or agent will probably need: a signed power of attorney (ideally notarised and bilingual BG/RO or EN), a copy of the owner's ID, the collector's own ID and driving licence, and the order number or VIN. Tesla RO should be told in advance through the advisor or support. Since the car is unregistered, the documents Tesla hands over (CoC, invoice) must reach the owner for NAP and KAT. The carrier should also check the car for damage on the owner's behalf, or the owner should accept that this check is being delegated.
- Moving an unregistered new car normally means a car carrier or Romanian transit plates. A carrier sidesteps the plate question.

### Gaps
- No primary Tesla Romania text on third-party collection (tesla.com was blocked). I recommend the buyer confirm the PoA format with their Tesla RO delivery advisor.
- No Romanian or Moldovan forum reports specific to Voluntari surfaced in search.
