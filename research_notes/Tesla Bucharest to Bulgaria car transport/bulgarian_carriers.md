# Bulgarian car-transport companies for a Tesla pickup in Bucharest/Voluntari with delivery to Bulgaria

Method note: research done 2026-10-07. WebFetch was blocked by the egress proxy for every carrier domain tried (kstrucks.bg, cars-help.com, evrotrans-petrov.com, web.repatrack.pro), so **all company details below come from search-engine snippets and summaries, not from reading the pages directly**. Phones and emails are reproduced exactly as the snippets showed them. Check them with the company before relying on them. Some site copy may date from before 2025. Several pages quote prices in BGN, which shows they were written before the euro changeover. No company page I found names Tesla Center Bucharest/Voluntari specifically.

## Which Bulgarian firms advertise Romania→Bulgaria car transport (автовоз Румъния / Букурещ / от Европа), or new cars and Teslas specifically?

### Takeaway
At least five Bulgarian companies say they carry cars from Romania, and one Ruse business is set up to import cars from Romania. The firms that mention Romania are KS Trucks (Sofia), Cars-Help (regular route through Romania), Евротранс-Петров 2017 (Montana) and cars-transport-bg.com (weekly runs to and from Romania). РУСЕ КАРС (Ruse) handles purchases of cars from Romania. ДАРИ ТРАНС 2013 and Ruven Trans are large or premium carriers that handle new cars, but no source I saw confirms they serve Romania. None of them advertises Tesla pickups.

## Which firms say they collect from dealers or showrooms, act on the owner's behalf, and handle CMR and insurance?

### Takeaway
No carrier page I could see says outright "we collect from the dealer under your power of attorney". РУСЕ КАРС comes closest: it sends a representative in Romania and handles the documents. A Bulgarian blog says Tesla lets a buyer hand over the car to a transporter they choose, using a power of attorney. CMR is the legal standard for international road transport, but I could not confirm any individual carrier's cargo insurance terms from the snippets.

### Cited Findings
- РУСЕ КАРС: sends a representative anywhere in Romania to inspect the car and prepare documents, and delivers to any address in Bulgaria — [Facebook /rusecars](https://www.facebook.com/rusecars/)
- A Bulgarian Tesla-owner and import source (search summary): on a Tesla purchase "you can receive a power of attorney and transport the vehicle through your chosen transportation method", and intermediaries charge service fees of 1000–3000 BGN excl. VAT. To drive the car from Romania to Bulgaria instead, you need Romanian transit plates (Romanian citizens only) or Bulgarian transit plates (proof of ownership and a certificate of origin required) — summary from results incl. [teslaowners.bg](https://teslaowners.bg/tesla-bgr-eood-balgariya-oficialno-predstavitelstvo/) and [kirichkov.com](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/). The search tool merged several sources here, so I cannot say which page gives which claim.
- kirichkov.com guide "Можем ли да си купим нова Tesla от Букурещ, Румъния?": you can buy an in-stock Tesla from the Bucharest showroom. You need transport "via platform or truck", a deposit of about €250 paid in RON, and Bulgarian VAT. The contract bars resale for 6 months — [kirichkov.com](https://kirichkov.com/959/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B5%D0%BC-%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%B4%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%B8%D0%BC-%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-tesla-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B1%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%89-%D1%80/)
- Context on CMR: international road carriage is governed by the CMR Convention (Geneva 1956) — [DHL BG FAQ](https://www.dhl.com/bg-bg/home/tovarni/tsentar-za-pomosht-za-evropeyski-suhopaten-i-zhelezopaten-transport/chesto-zadavani-vaprosi-za-evropeyski-suhopaten-transport.html). Carrier-liability (CMR) insurance covers loss or damage to cargo from acceptance to delivery — [OZK Insurance](https://www.ozk.bg/bg/otgovornost-za-prevozvacha/otgovornost-na-prevozvacha-crm)
- Licences: ДАРИ ТРАНС holds an international freight licence valid to 2029 — [dari-trans.com](https://dari-trans.com/). Cars-Help holds licence No. 22542 — [cars-help.com](https://cars-help.com/bg/uslugi/transportni-uslugi-avtovoz-cyala-evropa/)
- Closed carriers: ДАРИ ТРАНС advertises closed "VIP" platforms — [dari-trans closed transport](https://dari-trans.com/%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%BD%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%82-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%BB%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%BD%D0%B8-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%83%D0%BC-%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B8/). Евротранс-Петров runs open multi-car carriers (3 cars each) — [business-register.bg](https://www.business-register.bg/transportni-uslugi/item/6381-evrotrans-petrovi-prevoz-koli-platformi)

### Inferences
- Any licensed international carrier must issue a CMR consignment note for a Romania→Bulgaria trip. The buyer should still ask for proof of the carrier's CMR liability policy and its limit. The CMR default limit (8.33 SDR/kg) may not cover the full value of a new Tesla, so the buyer may need separate full-value cargo insurance.
- The driver will most likely need a signed authorisation from the buyer, and Tesla's own process for third-party handover. Ask both Tesla Bucharest and the carrier about this before booking.

### Gaps
- I found no explicit "collect from dealer with power of attorney" wording on KS Trucks, Cars-Help, Евротранс-Петров or cars-transport-bg.com pages, because the pages could not be fetched.
- Whether each carrier's insurance covers the full declared value is not published in any snippet.

## Published prices or rate examples for Bucharest→Bulgaria

### Takeaway
No company publishes a Bucharest→Bulgaria price. The published examples cover Western Europe→Bulgaria. Treat them as an upper bound, since Bucharest–Sofia is only about 400 km.

### Cited Findings
- KS Trucks: prices "start from 850 BGN (final price)" (≈ €435). Price depends mainly on km from the loading point and the fuel price — [kstrucks прices page](https://kstrucks.bg/%D1%83%D1%81%D0%BB%D1%83%D0%B3%D0%B8/%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B7-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D1%86%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B0/)
- ДАРИ ТРАНС 2013: Vienna→Sofia from 1650 BGN / €843.63. Munich→Sofia and Nuremberg→Sofia 1850 BGN / €945.89 (cars up to 1.5 t) — [dari-trans prices](https://dari-trans.com/%D1%86%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8-%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B7-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B8/). A Tesla Model 3 or Y weighs well over 1.5 t, so a surcharge is likely.
- Cars-Help: price only on written request. Send photos of the car and documents and the date it will be ready — [cars-help.com](https://cars-help.com/bg/uslugi/transportni-uslugi-avtovoz-cyala-evropa/)
- Aggregator guide (repatrack, publisher unclear): Germany, Netherlands or Belgium→Bulgaria costs €400–700 on a shared carrier and €600–1000 for a single car. Domestic bands: under 100 km €80–150, 100–300 km €120–220, over 300 km €180–350 — [web.repatrack.pro](https://web.repatrack.pro/bg/blog/cena-avtovoz)
- Tesla Model Y to Bulgaria: about €400 shared on an open truck, versus about €950 for private closed transport from Germany — [goelectric.bg 2026](https://www.goelectric.bg/blog/tesla-model-y-bulgaria-2026)
- VzemiKola (importer): "докарване от 850 лв." — [vzemikola.com](https://vzemikola.com/)

### Inferences
- From the distance-band figures, a single-car Bucharest→Sofia or Plovdiv trip would plausibly cost a few hundred euros. Ruse is closer and should be cheaper. This is an estimate only; no carrier has quoted it.
- Romanian road charges (rovinieta, plus the Danube bridge toll at Giurgiu–Ruse) and border queues at Ruse (6–16 h for trucks, per a parliamentary/ministry source) can add cost and delay — [capital.bg](https://www.capital.bg/specialni_izdaniia/kapital_gradove/2019/04/11/3409438_feribot_pod_mosta/) (2019 article; current queue times are unverified); [vintrica rovinieta](https://www.vintrica.com/bg/e-vinetka/rumieniia/); [Forbes BG on Romanian toll increase, Jul 2025](https://forbesbulgaria.com/2025/07/30/rumaniya-uvelichava-drastichno-patnite-taksi/)
