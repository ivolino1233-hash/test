# Market value of a 2022 Model Y Long Range AWD (October 2026)

Method note: researched 2026-10-12. WebFetch and curl could not reach any site from this environment (DNS failure on every host, including tesla.com, NHTSA, autoDNA and carVertical), so **every fact below comes from search-engine results and snippets, not from pages I opened**. Second research pass; the first pass ran out of shared search budget, so this pass re-checked or replaced its claims. Searches used: 28.

## Takeaway

I found 30 asking-price data points for used 2022 Model Y Long Range AWD cars across Romania, Bulgaria, Germany and other EU countries, all through search snippets. WebFetch was blocked, so I could not open any listing, and most listings show no posting date. For clean EU-spec 2022 LR AWD cars, asking prices cluster around €32–36k below 60k km, €30–35k at 60–100k km and €27–34k at 100–150k km. Above 150k km there are only two points (€25–31k). Damaged or US/Canadian-import cars on Bulgarian sites are listed at €11.9–24k and are not comparable. The market averages agree with this. mobile.de puts 2022 Model Y prices (all variants) at €32,692–35,982 ('Stand Oktober 2026'). AutoScout24.de gives a 2022 average of €34,575. AutoUncle.de's 2026 table gives a 2022 average of €34,877 (5th–95th percentile €29,188–39,992, median 62,200 km, 276 cars), down €3,180 in 12 months, which is about €265 per month. Values are falling. Autovista24 says BEVs keep the lowest share of list price of any powertrain (38.6%) and forecasts a further slight fall to Dec 2026. Two things push used prices down. New Juniper-era cars are cheap: the Model Y Standard is €39,990 in Germany (since autumn 2025), and the Standard Long Range RWD is €46,990 in Romania (2026). Germany cut the LR list price to €54,990 in Jan 2023 and to €49,990 in Jan 2024. Round 1's '€65,990→€53,990' was wrong; €65,990→52,990 were US dollar figures. I found no transaction (sold-price) data, DAT/Schwacke trade-in values or Tesla Romania used-inventory prices. Every figure here is an asking price. My own estimate of fair negotiated prices for a clean, EU-registered, Aug-2022 Shanghai LR AWD, with VAT included or margin-scheme, is: <60k km €31–34k; 60–100k km €29–32.5k; 100–150k km €26–30k; >150k km about €22–27k. Transaction prices are commonly around 5–10% below asking, but that is my judgement, not a sourced figure. Romanian listings marked 'NET'/'+TVA' must be grossed up for a private buyer. Bulgarian listings show dual EUR/BGN prices at the fixed rate of 1.95583, which I recomputed from two listings.

## Corrections to the first pass

- Round-1 said Tesla's Jan 2023 EU cuts took the German Model Y LR 'from about €65,990 to about €53,990'. That is unsupported and wrong. €65,990→52,990 were US dollar figures. German sources report the LR at €54,990 after the Jan 2023 cut (winfuture table: from €56,990; elektroauto-news: −€2,000) and €49,990 after a further €5,000 cut in Jan 2024 (electrive).
- Round-1 said 2025 German asking prices for 2022 LR AWD were 'roughly high-€20k to mid-€30k' (unverified). Current Oct-2026 data mostly support this. mobile.de shows €32,692–35,982 for 2022 Model Y; AutoScout24.de gives a 2022 average of €34,575; AutoUncle.de gives €34,877 (5th–95th percentile €29,188–39,992) at a median 62,200 km. Individual LR AWD asks run from €26,980 (143k km) to €34,990 (76–86k km).
- Round-1 said 'no 2026 figure can be stated from evidence'. That is now superseded: about 30 sourced 2026-index asking-price points across RO, BG, DE and other EU markets, plus aggregate indices, are listed in the findings.
- Round-1 said a 'cheaper Model Y Standard trim added in late 2025'. Confirmed for Germany: €39,990, orderable from autumn 2025. Romania also has a Model Y Standard Long Range RWD at €46,990 (2026).
- Round-1 said the 'Juniper refresh launched in Europe early 2025'. This is supported by indirect evidence: the Romanian launch was Feb 2025 from €44,900 (puterea.ro), and the Belgian Launch Series came in about €9,000 above the outgoing model before Tesla added standard trims at similar prices. I found no source quantifying the Juniper effect on used values.
- Round-1 said the 'Varna dealer tesla.mobile.bg sells used Tesla stock'. Confirmed. Current examples are an LR 4x4 built Feb 2023 at €35,739.30 / 69,900 лв, and a damaged LR 4x4 built Aug 2022 with about 129k km at €11,900 excluding VAT (listing dated 14 Aug 2026).

## Open questions

- What is this VIN's actual mileage, first-registration date, country of registration and seller type? Without these the price band can only be applied by mileage bucket.
- Is the asking price gross, net + TVA or margin scheme? A Romanian company seller changes the real cost by about €6–7k.
- Transaction (sold) prices: no DAT/Schwacke Händlereinkauf, auction (BCA/Openlane/Autorola) or carVertical/Indicata sold-price data was found. A paid DAT/Schwacke or Eurotax valuation by VIN would close this gap.
- Does Tesla sell used inventory in Romania (tesla.com/ro_RO/inventory/used), and at what prices for 2022 LR AWD cars? Not found via search.
- What is the current Romanian new price of the Model Y Premium (LR) AWD in 2026? Only the Standard LR RWD (€46,990) was found.
- Is there a measurable price difference between Shanghai-built (LRW) and Berlin-built (XP7) 2022 LR AWD cars in the EU used market, or for China-market grey imports? No data found.
- None of the listings was opened (WebFetch blocked), so posting dates, live status, exact variant and SoH claims are snippet-level. The Bulgarian 'Giga Berlin Dec-2022, 188,000 km at €30,790' figure looks inconsistent and needs checking.
