### 13. Ownership transfer in the Tesla app/account (private purchase) (medium confidence, sourced in this pass)

There are two routes. (1) The seller starts a transfer from their Tesla account: choose 'sold', recipient type 'Individual' (choosing 'Third Party' can leave the car in limbo). The buyer accepts through an email link and can create an account from it. (2) Tesla UK support: if bought through a third party, the buyer claims ownership in the app by VIN, and the previous owner may need to approve. The buyer uploads one photo ID (driver's licence or passport) and one proof of ownership, such as registration, title or bill of sale. Tesla's owner's manual says adding the car can take 3-5 business days. Third-party guides say app-to-app transfers can be nearly instant, while document problems can take more than 10 days.

- **This car:** Ask the seller to start the transfer and remove phone keys and key cards at handover. I found no EU or Bulgaria-specific list of accepted documents; a Bulgarian registration certificate plus sale contract is a reasonable expectation (unverified). Timing comes from a US manual.
- **Sources:** [tesla.com](https://www.tesla.com/en_gb/support/how-add-or-remove-vehicles-tesla-app), [tesla.com](https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modelx/en_us/GUID-F561C667-5BF1-4779-9C06-17F33C14E95A.html), [findmyelectric.com](https://www.findmyelectric.com/blog/how-to-transfer-tesla-ownership-explained/), [recharged.com](https://recharged.com/articles/how-to-transfer-tesla-ownership/)
